About Us

Youth for Cause Foundation was launched by kids from the Greater Toronto Area who wanted to support our everyday heroes—firefighters, police, healthcare workers, and waste management staff—who help keep our communities safe and running smoothly.

What began as a humble lemonade stand in the fall of 2022 to support firefighters battling devastating Alberta fires has grown into a community-powered movement. Our young founders wanted to do more, and today, we're proud to say that Youth for Cause empowers kids across Peel Region to lead with kindness and contribute to a better future.

How You Can Support Us

You can help our mission by donating, volunteering, or even purchasing from our shop. Every action you take supports children-driven initiatives—from making appreciation kits for frontline workers to hosting community cleanups, our impact grows with you.
