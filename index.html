Welcome to Youth for Cause

A youth-led foundation from the Greater Toronto Area, launched by kids who want to support community workers like firefighters who assist us in containing deadly fires, police, healthcare workers, and waste management workers.

About Us

The initiative started in the autumn of 2022 when a few kids opened a lemonade stand to support firefighters dealing with deadly fires in Alberta. Since then, it has grown into a volunteer program for kids across the Peel Region. Our future plans include supporting more communities and helping build a better country for future generations.

The foundation is currently led by Mesha Vaid, a 9-year-old student at Alloa Public School in Caledon.
