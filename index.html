Youth for Cause Foundation was launched by kids from the Greater Toronto Area who wanted to support community workers like firefighters, police officers, healthcare workers, and waste management staff, who assist us in times of crisis and keep our communities running smoothly.

About Us

The foundation began as an initiative by a few kids who decided to open a lemonade stand in autumn 2022 to raise funds for firefighters battling deadly fires in Alberta. Today, the foundation offers a volunteer program for kids across the Peel Region and aims to support communities and build a stronger country for future generations.

How You Can Support Us

You can support the Youth for Cause Foundation by donating towards our various programs. We focus on supporting community workers, cleaning our surroundings, and assisting local hospices. You can also register as a volunteer or purchase our apparel and accessories to help raise funds for these causes.

Our Programs

Our programs engage children and families in meaningful community service. From community clean-up events to appreciation kits for emergency workers, there are many ways you can get involved.

  • Community clean-up events
  • Support for frontline workers
  • Care packages for local hospices
  • Awareness campaigns and fundraising

Meet the Founder

The foundation is currently led by Mesha Vaid, a 9-year-old student at Alloa Public School in Caledon. Mesha, along with her friends, founded this initiative to make a positive impact on their community.
