About Us

Youth for Cause Foundation was created by a group of young dreamers in the GTA who wanted to support community heroes like firefighters, police officers, and healthcare workers. What started as a simple lemonade stand has grown into a passionate movement.

Today, we organize clean-up events, deliver care packages, and run fundraisers led by youth. It’s about compassion, leadership, and changing our world—one small act of kindness at a time.

How You Can Support Us

Donate. Volunteer. Share our story. Your support allows our kids to lead community projects, design thank-you kits for frontline heroes, and fund local charities. Every dollar and hour matters.
