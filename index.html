Welcome to Youth for Cause

A youth-led foundation from the Greater Toronto Area, launched by kids who care deeply about our community workers and environment. We support firefighters, police, healthcare workers, waste management, and local hospices through donations, clean-up programs, and community volunteering.

How You Can Help

You can support the foundation by donating, volunteering, or purchasing merchandise. All proceeds go directly into programs that help clean the environment and support our community heroes.
