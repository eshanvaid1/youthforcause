At Youth for Cause Foundation, we believe young people have the power to create meaningful change. Our volunteer program is designed to engage children and families in impactful community service.

Why Volunteer?

  • Make a difference in your community
  • Support frontline workers and local services
  • Help clean and protect the environment
  • Earn volunteer hours for school
  • Develop leadership and teamwork skills

Who Can Volunteer?

Our program is open to kids, youth, and families across the Peel Region. Whether you're 6 or 16, there's a way for you to get involved and support our mission.

Types of Opportunities

  • Community clean-up events
  • Appreciation kits for healthcare and emergency workers
  • Creating art and care packages for hospices
  • Helping run awareness campaigns
  • Assisting with lemonade stands and fundraisers

How to Get Started

Simply click the button below to register as a volunteer. A parent or guardian’s permission is required for minors.
