About Us

Youth for Cause Foundation was launched by a group of kids from the Greater Toronto Area who wanted to support community workers like firefighters, police, healthcare workers, and waste management workers. In autumn of 2022, a small group of children decided to open a lemonade stand to raise funds for firefighters who were dealing with the deadly wildfires in Alberta. This initiative sparked something much larger.

Today, the foundation operates volunteer programs for kids across Peel Region, and the plans for the future involve expanding support for communities and building a better country for future generations.

Our Programs

We focus on three core impact areas to bring long-term positive change:

🌿 Clean & Green

Organizing cleanup drives, planting trees, and raising awareness about sustainable practices in schools and communities.

💛 Care for All

Running donation drives for clothes, food, and hygiene kits. Supporting homeless shelters and families in crisis with essentials.

🚀 Youth Empowerment

Hosting leadership workshops, career mentoring sessions, and self-development programs to uplift young changemakers.
